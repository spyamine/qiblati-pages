Privacy policy

Qiblati is a free Qibla and prayer-times app. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. We don't collect any personal data: what the app needs stays on your device.

Location

Qiblati uses your location, while the app is open, to work out the direction of the Kaaba and your prayer times. These calculations happen on your device.

To show the name of your city, the app asks Apple's map service (the standard iOS geocoder) which place your coordinates belong to. Apple handles that request under its own privacy policy. Qiblati doesn't send your location anywhere else and doesn't store it outside your device.

Camera

The camera view uses Apple's ARKit to place the Kaaba in your surroundings. The image is processed on your device in real time. Qiblati never records, saves or uploads photos or video.

If you save a room, the app keeps a map of the room's visual features (an ARKit world map) on your device so it can recognise the room later. It isn't a photo, and it never leaves your device. Deleting the room, or the app, removes it.

Prayer times in Morocco

In Morocco, Qiblati downloads the monthly table of official prayer times from the website of the Ministry of Habous and Islamic Affairs. The request only names the month; it contains no location or personal data. Like any website, the Ministry's server sees your network address.

Notifications

Prayer and Qibla-day reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing is sent to a server.

Settings

Your preferences (language, prayer method, sounds and so on) are stored on your device, and in your device backups like any app's settings.

Children

Qiblati collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact
